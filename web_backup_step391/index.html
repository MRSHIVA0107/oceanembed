Physical Oceanographic Interpretation:

The downward depression of the 20°C and 26°C isotherms between 86°E and 90°E visualizes the core of the anticyclonic warm eddy. This subsurface thermal dome acts as a vast heat reservoir ($TCHP > 80\text{ kJ/cm}^2$) capable of fueling explosive tropical cyclone rapid intensification (RI) while minimizing wind-induced sea surface cooling.
