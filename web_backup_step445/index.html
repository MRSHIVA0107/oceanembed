How OceanEmbed Works: From Satellites to 3D Ocean Intelligence

A non-technical explanation of the breakthrough and operational value

1. THE PROBLEM

Satellites Are Blind Below the Surface

Satellites can only see the skin of the sea (top 1 mm to 1 cm). But deadly tropical cyclones and underwater acoustics depend on the heat stored tens of meters below. A sea that looks cool at the surface can hide a massive warm reservoir underneath!

2. OUR AI SOLUTION

Multi-Source Deep Learning Reconstructs the 3D Ocean

OceanEmbed combines multiple satellite observations: sea surface height bulges caused by warm eddies (SLA), sea temperature (SST), ocean currents, wind vectors, and sea surface salinity. Our ResNet-18 neural model "sees through" the surface to reconstruct temperatures down to 1000 meters.

3. OPERATIONAL VALUE

Predicting Cyclone Rapid Intensification (RI)

When Tropical Cyclone Heat Potential (TCHP) exceeds 80 kJ/cm², storms can explode into Category 4 or 5 super-cyclones overnight. OceanEmbed calculates real-time TCHP and warns port authorities and naval forces days before impact.
